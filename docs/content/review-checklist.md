# Client content review

Every public product, business fact, offer, policy, and brand relationship needs
client confirmation before publication. This checklist records items for client
approval; it is not evidence that a claim or product detail is correct.

## Business content

- Confirm the legal and trading names, ownership of phone/WhatsApp/email/social
  accounts, and the correct request recipients.
- Confirm service areas, delivery terms, support hours, and all public promises.
- Review product-brand partnerships, descriptions, certificates, specifications,
  and any claims about authenticity, delivery, stock, or response time.
- Approve the privacy notice and website terms for the browser-local quote list
  and external WhatsApp/email handoff.

## Products and images

- Add only client-approved products and variants through database operations.
- Confirm each exact brand, product type/category, colour/finish, size, pack,
  selling unit, price, tax wording, availability, minimum order, and offer dates.
- Use client-supplied photos that depict the exact product or variant and are
  approved for website use. When no image is supplied, the website uses a neutral
  placeholder.
- Keep unavailable items visible only if the client wants customers to see them,
  and display their actual availability status.

## Customer request journey

Visitors search the published catalogue, add products and quantities to a
browser-local quote list, review the request summary, then open WhatsApp or email
to send it. The site does not claim that an external message was sent or
delivered.
